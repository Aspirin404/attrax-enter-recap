✦ The Story

One Room. Endless Builders.

As part of the China AI Ecosystem Workshop, AttraX and Enter invited 50+ students from Tsinghua, PKU, and universities worldwide for a live Vibe Coding session. Sixty minutes. Real products. No excuses.

"When someone builds their first thing in 20 minutes, something clicks. They stop thinking about what they can't do — and start thinking about what they want to build next."

Students who had never shipped a line of production code were spinning up functional apps in under 20 minutes. This works. Right now. I just did it.
